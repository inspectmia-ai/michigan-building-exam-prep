How it works

Study mode gives immediate feedback and explanations. Exam mode hides correct answers until you submit or time expires. Questions and answer choices are randomized each session. Your study progress is stored locally in this browser. When the Google Sheets reporting URL is configured, completed session results are also submitted to the administrator.

For best offline access, keep this HTML file on your device and open it with Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari. No internet, account, or server is required.
